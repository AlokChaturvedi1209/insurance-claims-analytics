# Exploratory findings

These observations describe the reproducible synthetic dataset only; they are not insurer findings.

- The dataset contains 50,000 claims and an approval rate of 80.49% among closed claims.
- Verification has the highest simulated stage breach rate at 36.87%.
- `Missing documents` is the most common simulated rejection reason (2,018 claims).
- These patterns are hypotheses for investigation, not causal conclusions.
